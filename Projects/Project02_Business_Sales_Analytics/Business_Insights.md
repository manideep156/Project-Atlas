# 💡 Business Insights

## Project Overview

This document summarizes the business findings generated from the Sample Superstore dataset using Pandas.

---

## Revenue Insights

- Technology generated the highest category-level sales.
- Phones generated the highest sub-category sales.
- The Consumer segment generated the highest revenue.
- The West was the highest-revenue region.
- New York City generated the highest city-level sales.

---

## Profitability Insights

- Technology generated the highest category profit.
- Copiers generated the highest sub-category profit.
- Furniture generated the lowest category profit.
- Tables were the least profitable sub-category.
- Texas recorded the largest state-level loss.
- Discounts of 30% and above produced negative average profit.

---

## Customer Insights

- Consumer customers generated the highest total sales and profit.
- Corporate and Home Office should also be evaluated using profit margin, not only total revenue.
- Segment-specific pricing strategies may improve profitability.

---

## Geographic Insights

- The West was the strongest region by sales and profit.
- The South generated the lowest regional revenue.
- California generated the highest state-level profit.
- Texas recorded the lowest state-level profit.
- New York City was the strongest city by both sales and profit.

---

## Shipping Insights

- Standard Class was the most frequently used shipping mode.
- Same Day and Second Class had slightly higher average sales per record.
- First Class produced the highest average profit per record.
- Shipping mode comparisons should be interpreted carefully because shipment cost data is unavailable.

---

## Discount Insights

- Average discount was approximately 15.63%.
- The maximum discount was 80%.
- Profit generally decreased as discounts increased.
- Average profit became negative from the 30% discount level onward.
- High discounts should require additional profitability checks.

---

## Final Recommendations

1. Expand profitable Technology products.
2. Reassess Furniture pricing and costs.
3. Restrict excessive discounting.
4. Investigate state-level losses.
5. Replicate successful practices from the West.
6. Use profit margin alongside sales when evaluating performance.
