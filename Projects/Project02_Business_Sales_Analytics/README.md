# 🏆 Business Sales Analytics

## Overview

This project analyzes the Sample Superstore retail dataset using **Python and Pandas**.

The objective is to transform transactional sales data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, exploratory analysis, aggregation, KPI calculation, ranking, and profitability analysis.

---

## Business Questions

The project answers questions such as:

- Which categories generate the highest sales and profit?
- Which regions and states perform best?
- Which products or sub-categories create losses?
- How do discounts affect profitability?
- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
- Which business areas require management attention?

## Key Results

- Total Sales: **$2.30 million**
- Total Profit: **$286,241**
- Overall Profit Margin: **approximately 12.47%**
- Best Category: **Technology**
- Best Region: **West**
- Highest-Profit State: **California**
- Lowest-Profit State: **Texas**
- Strongest City: **New York City**
