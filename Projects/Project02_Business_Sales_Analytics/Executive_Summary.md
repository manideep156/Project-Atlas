# 📊 Executive Summary

## Business Objective

The objective of this project was to analyze retail sales data and identify the categories, customer segments, regions, states, cities, shipping modes, and discount strategies that influence revenue and profitability.

---

## Dataset Overview

The cleaned dataset contains **9,977 retail transactions** covering:

- 49 states
- 531 cities
- 4 regions
- 3 product categories
- 17 product sub-categories

The analysis was performed using Python and Pandas.

---

## Overall Performance

- **Total Sales:** $2,296,195.59
- **Total Profit:** $286,241.42
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 37,820
- **Average Discount:** 15.63%
- **Overall Profit Margin:** approximately 12.47%

---

## Key Findings

- Technology was the strongest category by both sales and profit.
- Phones generated the highest sub-category sales.
- Copiers generated the highest sub-category profit.
- Furniture produced comparatively weak profit despite strong sales activity.
- The West was the strongest region.
- California generated the highest state-level profit.
- Texas recorded the largest state-level loss.
- New York City generated the highest city-level sales and profit.
- Standard Class was the most frequently used shipping mode.
- Discounts of 30% and above were associated with negative average profit.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Increase investment in profitable Technology products.
- Review the Furniture category, especially Tables.
- Introduce controls for discounts above 20%.
- Investigate loss-making states such as Texas.
- Replicate successful strategies used in the West.
- Evaluate business performance using profit margin as well as sales.

---

## Final Conclusion

The company is profitable overall, but profitability is concentrated in specific categories, regions, and products. Technology and the West represent major strengths, while excessive discounting, Furniture performance, and state-level losses represent the main risks.

The strongest opportunity is to preserve sales growth while improving margins through better discount controls, product selection, and regional pricing strategies.
